# Nurox ERP - App Store Listing Preparation

Since Nurox currently relies on an offline-first **Progressive Web App (PWA)**, users can install the application directly from their mobile browsers (Safari/Chrome) without visiting an App Store.

However, when Nurox transitions to a wrapped React Native (Expo) architecture for native store distribution, use the following assets and copy.

## App Name

**Nurox: Next-Gen ERP**

## Subtitle

HR, Payroll, Sales & Projects

## Description

Manage your entire business on the go with Nurox ERP. Designed for modern B2B SaaS companies, Nurox brings the power of an enterprise suite directly to your pocket.

**Key Features:**

- **Offline Attendance**: Check in seamlessly with QR codes or biometrics, even when you lose internet connection.
- **Smart HR & Payroll**: Apply for leave, view payslips, and manage employee directories instantly.
- **Real-Time Push Notifications**: Never miss a manager approval, a ticket update, or an SLA breach alert.
- **Task Management**: Update project statuses and log time from anywhere.
- **AI-Powered Insights**: Ask the Nurox AI assistant questions about your business data in plain English.

_Note: You must have an active Nurox account provided by your organization to use this app._

## Privacy Policy

_To be hosted at `https://nurox.local/privacy`._

- We collect biometric data _locally only_ (FaceID/TouchID via WebAuthn) for secure login.
- Camera access is requested strictly for scanning attendance QR codes.
- Location data is collected during check-in for geofencing compliance (if enabled by the tenant admin).
